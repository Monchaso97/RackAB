RackAB - macOS
==============

INSTALL
1. Drag "RackAB.vst3" onto the "VST3 Plugins" shortcut in this window.
   (This copies it to /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3 - macOS may ask for
   your password once.)
2. Open your DAW (FL Studio, Pro Tools, etc.) and rescan plugins.

FIRST-RUN SECURITY STEP (only once)
Because this plugin is not notarized by Apple, macOS Gatekeeper may say it
"cannot be opened". Clear it once with EITHER method:

  a) In Finder, right-click RackAB.vst3 (in the VST3 folder) > Open, then
     confirm. -- or --

  b) Open Terminal and run:
     sudo xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/RackAB.vst3"

After that the DAW loads it normally.
